This POC uses a normalized transcription, not a facsimile edition. Sonnet 18 uses a semicolon after line 4, a comma after line 9, contractions ow'st/grow'st, lowercase death, and a comma after “this” in the final line. Sonnet 29 adds commas after lines 2 and 3. Apostrophes are straight; matching ignores letter case and otherwise requires the displayed punctuation. No modern annotations or translations included. Project Gutenberg's ebook 1041 was the initial reference, but its service was under maintenance during final verification.
